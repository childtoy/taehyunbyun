I’m a Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at <a href="https://www.korea.ac.kr">Korea University</a>, working with Professor Sungjoon Choi in the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/sungjoon-choi/home">Robot Intelligence Lab. (RILAB)</a>.

My doctoral research, **Toward Human-Grounded Humanoid Motion Synthesis**, connects generative modeling of human motion with controllable, human-like behaviors for humanoid robots.

I received my M.S. in Statistics from Korea University in **Seoul**, advised by Professor Myoungshic Jhun, and my B.S. in Information and Mathematics from Korea University in **Sejong**.

<h2 class="research-heading">Research Interests</h2>

I study **generative models**, including diffusion and flow matching, to learn human motion distributions and build controllable motion priors. Through **motion synthesis and retargeting**, I aim to translate these priors into natural movements that fit the embodiment and physical constraints of humanoid robots. I am also interested in human–robot interaction and in developing friendly, natural robot behaviors for everyday settings, including the home.
