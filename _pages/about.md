<span style='font-size: 1.3em;'>Hi, I'm Taehyun Byun</span><br>
I'm a Ph.D. student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at <a href='https://www.korea.ac.kr'>Korea University</a>, working in the <a href='https://sites.google.com/view/sungjoon-choi/home'>Robot Intelligence Lab. (RILAB)</a> under the supervision of Professor Sungjoon Choi. My research focuses on **fine-grained controllability in generative motion synthesis**, bridging theoretical AI with physical implementation through frameworks in conditional motion generation and diffusion models.

<span style='font-size: 1.3em;'>Research Interests</span><br>
My work centers on developing generative models that provide precise control over synthesized outputs. I explore diffusion models in function spaces (Hilbert space-based approaches), controllable motion generation and inbetweening, reinforcement learning for robotics, and human motion retargeting for humanoid robot control.
